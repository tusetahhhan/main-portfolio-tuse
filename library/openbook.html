Name: Hannae Wisdom of Forest
Architect: UnSangDong Architects
Region: Nowon-Gu, South Korea
Year: 2017
Intersecting gabled volumes symbolize a sense of togetherness, communication and community, and the separation of forms breaks up dominance of a single shape. The frames represent the landscape around the site where mountains, forests, and trees overlap. The internal layout is centered around a repeating shelving unit that forms the walls and designates space, whilst also providing a consistent element that flows throughout the building.
